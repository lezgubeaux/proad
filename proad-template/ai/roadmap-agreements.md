# Roadmap Agreements

## Purpose

Define the system-level agreements for how roadmaps are used and maintained in a PROAD .

## Agreements

- Every PROAD project should contain `project/roadmap.md`.
- The roadmap is mandatory and human-facing.
- The roadmap should remain minimal and should not become the project knowledge base.
- Roadmaps may use sub-steps when a top-level step contains meaningful internal progression.
- Sub-steps should represent real stages or outcomes, not every supporting file.
- Detailed plans, notes, research, implementation records, and similar supporting information belong in `docs/`.
- Supporting records may be linked from roadmap items when useful.
- Supporting records do not automatically become roadmap sub-steps.
- Uncertainty about roadmap position, meaning, or status must be resolved through questions.
- Structural changes to the roadmap require care and should not be made casually.

## Local Usage Contract

The local roadmap usage contract belongs in [`../project/roadmap.md`](../project/roadmap.md).

This file should define the broader agreement and should not duplicate the roadmap header in full.
