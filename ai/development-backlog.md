# PROAD Development Backlog

## Purpose

Store deferred, prospective, and unfinished improvements to the PROAD framework for later evaluation and development.

## Retrieval

- When the human asks to check the PROAD backlog, ideas, prospective features, unfinished features, deferred work, or possible next development, the AIA must consult this file.
- Follow links from relevant entries to their detailed records before describing or beginning that work.
- Backlog entries are candidates, not authorization to implement them.
- Before implementation, freshly inspect the current framework state and confirm that the stored idea still fits it.

## Maintenance

- Keep entries concise and link substantial ideas to dedicated records.
- Add an entry when an idea is useful enough to revisit but is not current approved work.
- Update an entry when its scope, status, or supporting record changes.
- Remove an entry or mark it completed when its intended outcome has been incorporated into live PROAD or deliberately rejected.
- Do not use this backlog for client-project tasks or ordinary roadmap work.
